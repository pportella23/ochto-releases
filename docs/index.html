Your machine, your logins

Ochto runs the official claude CLI, unmodified, for you to use. You sign in through Anthropic's own login, and Ochto never reads your tokens or Keychain. There is no Ochto account and no telemetry.

It keeps every account in its own folder and learns about your sessions from Claude Code's hooks and status line. Your ~/.claude and your own settings stay untouched.

Questions

What happens after the 7-day trial?

Your accounts, projects and history stay. Opening sessions needs a license; sessions already open keep running.

How many Macs can I use?

Two per license. You can free a Mac any time in Ochto › License… › Deactivate on this Mac.

Do I need a Claude subscription?

Yes. Ochto runs Claude Code, so each account needs Claude Code access (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise). Each account's usage is billed by Anthropic as usual.

What does Ochto send over the internet?

Only two things: your license key and its activation id, to activate and check the license with our payment provider, and an update check against GitHub. Never your prompts, code, accounts or usage.

Is this allowed by Anthropic?

Ochto runs the unmodified Claude Code CLI, and you sign in with your own subscription through Anthropic's login. You are responsible for using each account within Anthropic's terms.

Refunds?
